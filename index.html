How it works

Three taps, then put your phone down.

Pick what matters

Choose two or three goals: read more, move more, be mindful, connect with people, or learn a language.

Say how long you’ve got

1, 5, 15 or 30+ minutes, and where you are. No squats suggested while you’re standing in line.

Do one small thing

Start the timer and pocket your phone. It buzzes when time’s up, and the minutes count toward your day.

What’s inside

Built to beat the scroll reflex.

Before you scroll…

Hook it up to Instagram or TikTok with iPhone Shortcuts and PocketMoment opens first: one slow breath, then a better option. Keep scrolling if you want; the choice is yours.

Suggestions that know you

Add the book you’re reading, the people you want to stay close to, and articles you’ve saved. “Read five pages of Shoe Dog” beats “read something.”

A ring worth filling

Set a daily goal of 5 to 30 minutes and watch the ring fill. Streaks, a week at a glance, and small celebrations at 3, 7 and 30 days.

Links that go somewhere

Poems of the day, guided meditations, short stories, workouts, language clips, picked around your interests so the next step is one tap away.

The math

Ten minutes a day is about 61 hours a year. That’s roughly a dozen books you could have read instead.

Private by design.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your goals, streaks and saved stuff live on your phone and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy.
