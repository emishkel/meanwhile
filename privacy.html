Privacy policy

Your data stays on your phone.

Last updated September 30, 2026

The short version

Meanwhile has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We don’t collect, sell or share personal information. Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Meanwhile saves the following locally on your device:

  • Your goals, interests, language choice and daily goal
  • Your progress: completed moments, minutes, streaks and history
  • Anything you add under “Your stuff”: the book you’re reading, names of people you want to keep in touch with, and links you save
  • Your settings, such as reminder time and where-you-are preference

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, or use Settings → Reset all data, it is erased.

What we collect

Nothing. Meanwhile does not collect personal data, usage analytics, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or crash reports, and does not use third-party tracking or advertising tools.

News headlines

If you pick interests such as Markets or Science, the app may show a current headline from a public news feed (for example The Wall Street Journal, Quanta Magazine or Smithsonian). To do this, your phone requests that feed directly from the publisher. The request contains no personal information from the app, though, like any web request, the publisher can see your device’s IP address. Headlines are cached on your device for an hour. Some publishers’ articles may require a subscription.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders or use the activity timer, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on your phone, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone’s Settings.

Links to other websites and apps

Some activities include links to third-party sites and apps (for example, articles, videos or language apps). When you open one, you leave Meanwhile and that service’s own privacy policy applies. We don’t control and aren’t responsible for their practices.

Shortcuts integration

If you set up the optional “Doomscroll interceptor”, you create the automation yourself in Apple’s Shortcuts app. Meanwhile only counts, on your device, how many times it was opened that way each day. It cannot see which apps you use.

Children

Meanwhile is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. If we ever start collecting any data, we’ll say so here first and ask for your consent in the app where required.
