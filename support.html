Support

How can we help?

Quick answers first. If you’re still stuck, email us and a real person will reply.

How do I get a suggestion?

On the home screen, pick where you are (optional), then tap 1, 5, 15 or 30+ minutes. Swipe the card, or tap “Try another”, to see a different idea.

My reminders or “time’s up” alerts aren’t showing.

PocketMoment needs permission to send notifications. On iPhone, go to Settings → Notifications → PocketMoment and turn on Allow Notifications. Then choose a reminder time again in the app under Settings → Daily reminder.

Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can also hide them.

How do I set up the doomscroll interceptor?

In the app, open Settings → Doomscroll interceptor and follow the steps. In short: in Apple’s Shortcuts app, create an Automation for when Instagram, TikTok or another app is opened, set it to Run Immediately, and add an “Open URLs” action with the link shown in PocketMoment.

To pause it, switch the automation off in Shortcuts.

How do I change my goals, interests or daily goal?

Tap the gear on the home screen. “Goals, interests & your stuff” changes what suggestions are drawn from, and “Daily goal” changes how many minutes fill your ring.

Why don’t I see news headlines?

Headlines appear on some reading moments when you’ve picked a matching interest (Markets & finance, Business, Tech, World news, Science, History, Psychology, Philosophy, Travel or Sports) and you’re online. If a news source is slow or unavailable, you’ll see the regular links instead.

Why did my streak reset?

A streak counts days in a row with at least one completed moment. Missing a full day starts a fresh one. Your best streak is always saved on the progress screen.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. Everything is stored only on your device, so deleting the app or switching phones starts fresh. Backup is on our list.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Reset all data, or delete the app. We don’t hold any copy of your data. See the privacy policy.
